Mediaro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Mediaro is a local medication reminder application. Your health data stays on your device. We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal health information to any third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Google Sign-In provides your name and email address for authentication purposes only.
  • Medication Data: Medicine names, dosages, schedules, and frequencies you enter.
  • Health Records: Blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, SpO2 readings, and other vitals you manually record.
  • Appointment Data: Doctor names, specialties, and appointment dates you create.
  • Profile Information: Your name and dependent profiles you create.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Basic device model and Android version for app compatibility.
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash data (if enabled) to improve app stability.

2. How We Store Your Data

All your health data is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. No health data is transmitted to external servers.
  • Medications, vitals, appointments, and profiles are stored in encrypted local storage on your device.
  • Data is not backed up to cloud services.
  • Data is not synchronized across devices.
  • If you uninstall the app, all local data is permanently deleted.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google Sign-In

  • We use Google Sign-In for authentication.
  • Google may collect usage data as per their Privacy Policy.
  • We only receive your name and email for account creation.

3.2 Firebase

  • Firebase is used for authentication and anonymous analytics.
  • Firebase may collect device information as per Firebase Privacy.

4. What We Do NOT Do

  • ❌ We do NOT sell your data to anyone.
  • ❌ We do NOT share your health information with advertisers.
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location.
  • ❌ We do NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal files.
  • ❌ We do NOT send your health data to any server.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Important: Mediaro is a medication reminder tool only. It is NOT a medical device and does not:
  • Diagnose any medical condition
  • Provide medical advice
  • Recommend treatments
  • Replace professional medical consultation

Always consult a certified healthcare professional before making any changes to your medication regimen.

6. Data Security

  • Data is stored locally using Android's secure storage mechanisms.
  • We do not transmit sensitive health data over the internet.
  • App access is protected by your device's screen lock.

7. Children's Privacy

Mediaro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app.
  • Delete: Uninstall the app to permanently delete all local data.
  • Export: You can generate PDF reports of your health data from within the app.
